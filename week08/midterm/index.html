About this project

Concept:

A surrealist story about facing the difficulties of being a student. Users will follow along a slightly interactive story over two pages - Part One and Part Two - and have the opportunity to interact with input divs.

Users assume the role of the narrator. You walk around Hunter and realize it is totally empty. New doors appear where there previously were none and each door branches into several others. Each room you pass through represents a frustration, anxiety, or insecurity that comes with being a student.

Being a student is difficult. Our academic responsibilities mutually impact our personal relationships and self-worth. Sometimes it seems insurmountable. We are capable of reaching graduation. The self-fulfillment and strength gained through it build our endurance.

Influences:

    House of Leaves by Mark Danielewski
    i just woke up... by Aya Takao
    love me or love me not by Alicia Guo"
Charley McLean's Web Production I Midterm Project, Hunter College Fall 2024
