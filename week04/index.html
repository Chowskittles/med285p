Welcome! Learn more about Olympic National Park

Olympic National Park has a rich history and breathtaking scenery. From glaciers and temperate rainforests to banana slugs and killer whales, this (literally and figuratively!) cool national park has something for everyone to enjoy.

The purpose of this site is to give you a rough idea and feeling for the park. There is more to the park than I can cover in a simple website, so let's go through some highlights.
