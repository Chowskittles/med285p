Final Project Proposal

The Lost Galaxy

Final Project Proposal I will make a narrative-driven website about a “Lost Galaxy” that was recently discovered by NASA. The site will function as a top-secret archive that explains the discovery and history of the Lost Galaxy and its political importance. Users will be notified that the website should only be accessed by authorized NASA and Department of Homeland Security personnel. The website will have three pages: the index, an about section, and a planet generator. The index functions as a home page that introduces users to the Lost Galaxy concept and notes that accessing the archive as a non-NASA or DHS employee is a punishable crime. The about section delves into the lore of the Lost Galaxy and encourages users to move on to the planet generator page. The planet generator will be a p5.js canvas with a button that generates a new planet each time the button is clicked. There will be a planet, planetary features inside the radius of the planet (they will not generate past the boundaries of the planet), and stars that generate around the planet. The planetary features and stars will be array elements and will generate in random areas (the stars generate outside the planet while the planetary features generate within the planet). The planet itself will be a random size every time but will not exceed the limits of the canvas or be smaller than 100px.
