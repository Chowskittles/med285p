You wake up on one of the sky bridges leading to Hunter West

It is night time and nobody else is around. Why are you on campus, anyway?

Regardless, you're here now.

You look around and realize all the hallways have been walled off. The atmosphere is eery and you walk down the stalled escalators to get to the lobby. A large black door is set where the exit normally is. There seems to be no other way out. The door creaks open and you decide to go through.

As soon as you pass through the door, it disappears from behind you as if it were never there. This room is dark and hazy. Something keeps your footsteps from producing a sound even though the floor is hard. You instinctively try to shudder, but any noise you try to voice is muffled into near silence.

You can't help but think:

Four doors are lined next to each other along the wall in front of you. Each of them has a huge lock except the one on the far left. You decide to go through.

You enter a room with a red curtain. It feels hotter than the hottest summer day you have ever experienced, and it is quickly getting hotter.

You push past the curtain and see another four doors 100 feet ahead of you. Now the second from the left is unlocked. The heat is intense, but you can handle it for that long. Something lights up inside of you and you dash with all your might, flinging the door open and quickly shutting it behind you.

Water starts pouring into the room from waves beyond the walls. These walls don't allow you to reach into the endless sea. You frantically look around for a way out. Another four doors are in this room. This time, the middle door is unlocked. However, these ones are already underwater. The room is filling and you will drown if you don't hurry.

You did it! You pull the door open and swim through.

You fall to the ground and feel course grains. Wind is rushing around you and a sandstorm keeps you from seeing anything at all.

You pull your shirt over your nose and mouth and begin to focus. Breathe...you've got this. For some reason, you know exactly where to go. Trust your instincts.

You trudge your feet through the sand for five minutes with your hand extended forward. Not every step is taken with confidence but you decide to maintain faith in yourself. It pays off. You feel another doorhandle and twist.

An empty void consumes everything in this room except for a solitary door far ahead. This door is massive. It is larger than any building you have ever seen. This room has no ceiling, no walls, and no floor - yet you manage to stand. No structure - that's how you felt in the other rooms.

Your bravery is worth the risk. The door grows smaller as you approach it. Something that was insurmountably huge is coming together just right for you. Finally, you reach the door and twist the handle. You can hear cheering and feel the warmth of daylight touch your skin.
