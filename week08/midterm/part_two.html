Being a student is an immense challenge.

Between coursework, personal relationships, deadlines, and time management, it's easy to feel like everything is on fire. Tensions rise and the heat turns up as the semester rolls along.

Things pile up and it feels like you're drowning.

Sometimes it feels impossible to navigate. The sheer difficulty and volume can blind and deafen you from the things you need to see or hear.

It can feel like you're climbing Everest - a true uphill battle.

Keep going. It isn't a marathon. Believe in yourself.
School is incredibly difficult. Of course, you know this. If you made it into college in the first place, you are more than capable of making it through to the end.
Stand proud and know you can do this.
